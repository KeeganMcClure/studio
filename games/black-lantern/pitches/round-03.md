## A · LARKSPUR (back from round 2)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Hollins Ferry, Ohio, a river town in late October. One big Victorian house on Larkspur Street, a church on the hill, and a bus depot that only runs twice a day |
| Hook | Ilona Brandt, a widow, kept a diary for the last year of her life. Her nephew Danny found it when he came to clear out her house, and he read the first page. Now his phone rings at night with his aunt's voice, his car won't start, and every bus out of town is cancelled on the day he buys a ticket. Seven pages of the diary are written in, and Danny swears it won't let you read more than one a day. Big Hat wants the diary, the house and Danny, in that order. |
| Monster theme | A gift that wants paying for |
| Night treatment | The house after dark: every mirror covered with a sheet, a few fewer sheets each night, and the church bell ringing at hours nobody rings it |
| Heaviest themes | Grief, suicide, abuse (off screen), obsession |
| Open or quiet | Quiet: the agents come in as appraisers for the bank that holds the house |
| Seeds used | The patron's house (every name changed), its seven-day diary and the leaving-town rule |

## B · LOST HOUR (new)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Cullen Gap, Virginia, in April. A two-lane mountain road through the Blue Ridge, a scenic overlook with a broken coin viewer, a diner that closes at eight, and a small-town sleep clinic in an old bank building |
| Hook | Ray and Donna Pruett drove home from their daughter's wedding along the ridge road. They left the reception at ten and pulled into their driveway at a quarter past three, with a full tank they hadn't filled, a scorched ring on the hood and no memory of the hours between. Since then Donna can't sleep without the lights on, Ray has a lump behind his ear the doctor won't explain, and a hiker found their wedding card on the overlook, a hundred feet off the road, folded into a shape nobody can unfold. Big Hat wants the missing hours back, and the lump out, before the couple start talking to the paper. |
| Monster theme | Visitors who take people apart to see how they work, and put them back almost right |
| Night treatment | Spring fog on the ridge: headlights that die a few feet out, peepers screaming in the hollows, and lights over the trees that don't blink like planes |
| Heaviest themes | Medical horror, a marriage under strain, paranoia, a hard choice about people who did nothing wrong |
| Open or quiet | Quiet: the agents come in as researchers from a sleep study the couple signed up for |
| Seeds used | Classic abduction (every name changed): the lost night, hypnosis, the medical exam and the implant |

## C · TRACKING (back from round 2)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Reno, Nevada, in winter. A pawn shop on Fourth Street, a closed-down nightclub, and a motel that rents by the week |
| Hook | A VHS tape turned up in a pawn shop's five-for-a-dollar bin with "WE KNOW" written on the label. The clerk watched it on a Tuesday. By Friday he'd covered his windows in foil and started writing the same three words on everything he owned. The tape shows a dancer at a club that closed in 1996, sick and covered in sores, moving faster than a person should, while someone off camera chants. Big Hat wants every copy found, and wants to know why the dancer on the tape still looks the same in a photo taken last month. |
| Monster theme | A curse passed hand to hand, like a tape you shouldn't have watched |
| Night treatment | Neon and snow, the casino signs bleeding colors across wet streets, the tape's tracking lines creeping into the edges of the screen as the case goes on |
| Heaviest themes | Addiction, exploitation (off screen), body horror, self-harm |
| Open or quiet | Quiet: the agents work as collectors buying up old tapes |
| Seeds used | "WE KNOW" tape |

## Notes for the pick

- **Variety so far.** Two summer cases in a row (Good Manners, Lot 83) after one autumn. **A** is autumn, **B** would be the season's first spring case, **C** its first winter one. Later rounds keep the seasons and regions mixed.
- **What each leans on:** **A** on History, Occult and Empathy, with a clock you can't hurry; **B** on Medicine, Empathy and Science, with a night drive and a hypnosis scene as its set pieces; **C** on Computers, History and Occult with a trail to follow.
- **Art cost (Season readiness, step 6):** **A** is the cheapest: an autumn small town that reuses most of today's Shared Shelf, with a Victorian house, a church and interiors as the new pieces. **B** needs a spring kit, a mountain road with an overlook, and a clinic interior; the spring kit would serve later spring cases too. **C** needs a snowy city at night and indoor spaces, the most new art, and its winter kit would serve later winter cases.
- **Length:** B and C fit one case. A is flagged above.
- **Seeds left after this round:** Bad pregnancy, plus whichever two of these aren't picked. The Missing agent seed is held back, since a case about the agency's own people may clash with the secret agency arc; the Developer can use it there if it fits.
