## Notes for the pick

- **Variety so far.** Season 1 has an autumn, two summers and a spring, and only one open case (Good Manners). **B** and **C** are both winter, so either gives the season its first. **B** is the only open case here, which would balance the three quiet ones.
- **What each leans on:** **A** on History, Occult and Empathy, with a clock you can't hurry; **B** on Bureaucracy, Medicine and Forensics, with a paper trail through a closed clinic; **C** on Computers, History and Occult with a trail to follow.
- **Overlap to weigh:** **B** shares a medical side with Lost Hour (a body something was done to). Its feel is different (an open paper chase in the desert, not a quiet night drive), but if two medical cases feel like too many in one season, **C** is the cleaner pick.
- **Art cost (Season readiness, step 6):** **A** is the cheapest: autumn small town, most of today's Shared Shelf. **B** needs a desert winter kit (adobe walls, mesa, lantern bags, a trailer park) and reuses the interiors and parking lot from the summer kits. **C** needs a snowy city at night, the most new art.
- **Length:** B and C fit one case. A is flagged above.
- **Seeds left after this round:** whichever two of these aren't picked. Round 5 will need at least one new seed or a reworked one; the Missing agent seed is still held back for the agency arc.
