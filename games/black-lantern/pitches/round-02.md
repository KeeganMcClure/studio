## Notes for the pick

- **Variety after Good Manners.** Good Manners is open, in daylight, and led by talk (Persuade, Empathy, Bureaucracy). All three options here are quiet cases, so the team works under a cover and overstepping starts at a different step.
- **What each leans on:** **A** on Search, Science and Stealth in tight indoor spaces; **B** on History, Occult and Empathy, with a clock you can't hurry; **C** on Computers, History and Occult with a trail to follow.
- **Art cost (Season readiness, step 6):** **B** is the cheapest, since it's an autumn small town and reuses most of today's Shared Shelf, with a Victorian house, a church and interiors as the new pieces. **A** needs an apartment block and summer pieces, much of which Good Manners' summer kit could share. **C** needs a snowy city at night and indoor spaces, the most new art.
- **Length:** A and C fit one case. B is flagged above.
