## A · LOT 83

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Austin, Texas, in August. A tired two-story apartment block, the Pecan Court, with a dry pool and a laundry room that's always running |
| Hook | Walt Okafor buys dead strangers' estate lots and resells what he can. His newest lot came from a collector nobody in the family will talk about: boxes of jars, books in no language Walt knows, and a run of numbered notebooks with one missing. Three days after he opened the boxes, the pest-control company quit the job at his building, the neighbor's dog stopped barking at his door, and Walt stopped answering his phone. Big Hat wants the lot found, counted and gone before the building's other twenty-three units notice anything. |
| Monster theme | Something small that came in a box and won't stay small |
| Night treatment | A heat wave that doesn't break after dark: cicadas, sodium lights, a pest-control tent over the building like a circus top |
| Heaviest themes | Grief and loneliness, body horror, a death the party may not prevent |
| Open or quiet | Quiet: the agents come in as the pest-control crew |
| Seeds used | Apt# 432 (renamed), Flavor: the glassy-eyed neighbor with the dog, the shrieking ceiling fan, the grandkid's drawing on a dead man's fridge |

## B · GOOD MANNERS

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Odessa Flats, Kansas, a grain town of 900 between two highways, during the county fair's last week |
| Hook | For a month, two men in black suits and dark glasses have been turning up around Odessa Flats. They stand across the street from someone's house and watch for hours. When they do talk, they sound like they learned English from a manual. Three of the people they watched have since died in their sleep, and the sheriff is calling it a prank and a bad flu season. Big Hat sends the agents in with badges: find out who the men are and what they want, and stop it before anyone else dies. |
| Monster theme | Things practicing how to be people |
| Night treatment | Empty streets under one blinking light, the fairground's rides lit and turning with nobody on them, a tinted car that follows for an hour and then isn't there |
| Heaviest themes | Stalking and being watched, death of innocents, paranoia |
| Open or quiet | Open: the agents arrive with badges, so the sheriff knows them from the first scene |
| Seeds used | Men in black (renamed), Flavor: the tinted black car, the sketchy rural gas station |

## C · TRACKING

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Reno, Nevada, in winter. A pawn shop on Fourth Street, a closed-down nightclub, and a motel that rents by the week |
| Hook | A VHS tape turned up in a pawn shop's five-for-a-dollar bin with "WE KNOW" written on the label. The clerk watched it on a Tuesday. By Friday he'd covered his windows in foil and started writing the same three words on everything he owned. The tape shows a dancer at a club that closed in 1996, sick and covered in sores, moving faster than a person should, while someone off camera chants. Big Hat wants every copy found, and wants to know why the dancer on the tape still looks the same in a photo taken last month. |
| Monster theme | A curse passed hand to hand, like a tape you shouldn't have watched |
| Night treatment | Neon and snow, the casino signs bleeding colors across wet streets, the tape's tracking lines creeping into the edges of the screen as the case goes on |
| Heaviest themes | Addiction, exploitation (off screen), body horror, self-harm |
| Open or quiet | Quiet: the agents work as collectors buying up old tapes |
| Seeds used | "WE KNOW" tape |

## Notes for the pick

- All three fit in 1–2 hours as one case; none needs a Part 2.
- Each aims at different team strengths: **A** leans on Search, Science and Stealth in tight indoor spaces; **B** on Persuade, Empathy and Bureaucracy with a town that talks; **C** on Computers, History and Occult with a trail to follow.
- None of them is autumn, so each would be the first case to ask for new art beyond today's Shared Shelf pieces (Season readiness, step 6): an apartment block and a city street (A), a Kansas grain town and a fairground (B), a snowy city at night and indoor spaces (C).
