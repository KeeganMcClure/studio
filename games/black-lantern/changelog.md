| Nov 21, 2026 | Bug | Walking speed on fast screens (left over from spec 001, which sent it straight to the Developer as a bug). Walking counted its 10-frame steps in screen frames, so the lead walked twice as fast on a 120 Hz phone (ProMotion iPhones, many Androids), 1.5× on 90 Hz, and half speed on 30 Hz (iPhone Low Power Mode). Measured, one step took 84 ms at 120 Hz and 333 ms at 30 Hz, against about 167 ms on the iPhone SE. Now every screen takes about 167 ms a step (measured 164 to 168 ms at 30, 60, 90, 120 and 144 Hz), which is spec 001's "10 frames, about a sixth of a second at 60 Hz". **How:** new shared `engine/clock.js` counts 1/60-second ticks from the screen's own timestamps. The game loop runs its logic once per tick and draws when at least one tick has passed, so a 120 Hz screen draws every other frame and a 30 Hz screen runs two ticks per frame. A pause longer than ¼ second (a hidden tab or a stall) counts as one tick, so the game never jumps ahead. Battle animations (the background wave, damage numbers floating up, enemy fades) also count frames, so they are now 60 Hz speed on fast screens too. **iPhone SE (60 Hz) is unchanged:** a test of 6,000 frames with up to 2.5 ms of timing wobble gave exactly one tick on every frame, so play is the same frame for frame. Rushlight gets the same fix (its `engine.js` loop is still a fork, so the two-line loop change was copied by hand). Checks: `npm run check` passes in Black Lantern and Rushlight at 320 × 480, 375 × 550 and 375 × 667, and the launcher check passes. Saves untouched. Also updated: `src/CLAUDE.md` (the parts list). Left: on a 30 Hz screen battle animations still play at half speed, as before (they are drawn once per screen frame; only walking was the bug). Deviations: none |
