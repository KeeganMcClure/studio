# Spec 011: Lot 83

Status: proposed, waiting for Keegan's merge · Written 2026-11-22 by the Game Designer · Game: Black Lantern · To do: Lot 83 part A and part B

## In plain words

Walt Okafor lives at the Pecan Court, a tired two-story apartment block in Austin, Texas, in an August heat wave. He buys dead strangers' estate lots and resells what he can. His newest lot came from a collector nobody in the family will talk about: boxes of jars, books in no language Walt knows, and a run of numbered notebooks with one missing. Three days after he opened the boxes, the pest-control company quit the job at his building, the neighbor's dog stopped barking at his door, and Walt stopped answering his phone. Big Hat sends the agents in as the new pest-control crew: find the lot, count it, and get it out before the building's other twenty-three units notice anything.

What it feels like to play: Good Manners' opposite. It's a quiet case in tight indoor spaces, under a cover that the tenants can see through if you push too hard. Search, Science and Stealth carry it. The first half is a day of "inspections", knocking on doors and counting jars. Then the building is tented for the night, the tenants are moved out, and the agents are shut inside with whatever is loose in it. The dread is small and close: noises in the walls, things missing from the count, a building that's too quiet.

Picked pitch: [`pitches/round-02.md`](../pitches/round-02.md), option A, picked Nov 22, 2026.

**Spoiler rule:** this spec gives only structure and mechanics. It never says what came in the box, what happened to Walt, what the missing notebook holds, or how it ends. That goes in the secret case file (Story → Adding a new case, step 5).

## The case at a glance

| Piece | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 1–2 hours, one case |
| Open or quiet | **Quiet.** The agents come in as the pest-control crew, so Overstepping's first step is **Noticed**, then Local chief. "Who are these guys?" is the case's running pressure: the manager, a nosy tenant and the dog all watch the crew |
| Day and night | **One day and one long night.** Day 1 (afternoon) is the inspection: knocking on doors, the manager's office, Walt's unit, the laundry room. A story beat at dusk tents the building and moves the tenants to a motel, which turns it to night. Night 1 is the rest of the case, inside the tent. **A soft clock:** the tent comes down at dawn and the tenants come back. Time spent (a long search, resting, backtracking for a missed unit) costs a better ending, never progress |
| Points of no return | `studio-bible/play.md`; Systems → Gameplay loop, Turning the day. **Tenting the building at dusk** waits for Go on / Not yet: it closes **Walt's storage unit** (day only) and every tenant who leaves for the motel, so its line names them. The boss waits for you as usual. The case file lists anything else it closes |
| Maps | **Pecan Court** (the courtyard with the dry pool, the two-story walkway and stairs, the parking lot and the laundry room): day and night, with the tent on at night. **Interiors:** Walt's unit, the manager's office, and 3 other units the case file picks, each small. **Walt's storage unit** (a self-storage row across the highway, reached by van): day only. **The finale area**, which the case file names and places |
| Story fights | **2, both unnatural.** One small (1 skull, a swarm-type fight that teaches the case's enemy), one larger (2 skulls). Plus the boss. No human fight, since the cover keeps the tenants out of it |
| Danger zones | **1:** the tented building on night 1. 2–3 roamers in the dark halls and walkway, each guarding something (a clue, a shortcut, a person who stayed behind, the Lantern Box). Stealth can slip past them; the tent's dark is the danger zone's look |
| Boss | 3 skulls. At least one case choice weakens it (by kind: what you counted and what you did with it, as the case file sets), and a weakness Identify can find. Target 8–12 rounds against a careful party |
| Clue style | **Clues as keys.** About 6–7 clues, each opening a specific place or line of dialog (a unit, the storage row, a tenant who'll talk, the finale area). The lot itself is an inventory to count: what's in it and what's missing are the case's keys. The case file sets which key opens what, and always gives a second way into anything the story needs, so the deduction never dead-ends |
| Deduction | One deduction before the finale. A wrong answer costs time or a better ending, never progress (Gameplay loop) |
| Skills it calls on | Search, Science, Stealth, Occult, History, Forensics, Medicine, Empathy, Persuade, Craft. Every team template must be able to reach the finale |
| Motivation moments | 2: one for **Curiosity**, one for **Family** |
| Horrors | About 3 Unsettling or Disturbing, 1–2 Severe, 1 Shattering, and the Boss, inside the case budget (6–10 net SAN). Kinds: mostly unnatural and violence (body horror). A **phobia subject** fits the case's small things; the case file names it |
| Overstepping | Fewer choices overstep than in Good Manners, but the cover is thin: a choice that breaks the cover (forcing a door with tenants home, a scene in the courtyard) marks someone saw. Inside the tent at night, most choices are unseen. The **extreme case flag** for Local chief arriving as a fight with deputies is **off** |
| Cover stories | 2 strong ones (Persuade and Bureaucracy) plus the weak Luck story. Since it's a quiet case, a clean run also offers the easy one (Overstepping and cover stories). Clean books adds its safe one as always |
| Ending labels | `clean`, `costly`, `worst` (Federal agents' shutdown also plays `worst`) |
| Lantern Boxes and loot | 1 Lantern Box (2–3 catalog items). A loot table of 5 field items that fit an apartment block and a storage unit. Up to 3 case-only items, at least one unnatural, so bag or hide has a real choice. A **new generic item** (a pest-control fogger) is a candidate for the shared item list, if the case file uses it |
| Requisition | As usual at the briefing |

## Art

August in Austin: hot white sky by day, sodium orange and bug-light purple by night, cicadas implied by the light, not shown. At night the building sits under a striped fumigation tent, so the night map is darker and closer than the day one, lit by work lights and the laundry room's glow. **No autumn pieces** (pumpkins, hay bales, autumn trees, falling leaves) from the Shared Shelf.

- **From the Shared Shelf (v1):** the sprite method and person template, street light, sidewalk, grass, walls (brick and siding), roofs, windows, doors, awning, dusk lighting.
- **Shared with Good Manners if they're on the Shelf first:** summer grass and trees, the parked car. The art kit list (Season readiness, step 6) proposes these.
- **New for this case**, on its own Asset Library page made from the Still Harvest one:
  - **Pecan Court:** a two-story block with an open walkway and metal stairs, numbered doors, window AC units, a dry pool with a cracked bottom, a chain-link fence, the parking lot, the apartment sign (look-alike name), a pest-control van (look-alike name), and the striped tent that covers it all at night.
  - **Interiors:** Walt's unit (boxes everywhere, the shrieking ceiling fan, the grandkid's drawing on the fridge), the manager's office, 3 small units with one detail each, the laundry room.
  - **The storage row:** roll-up doors, a gravel lot, one buzzing light.
  - **The lot:** jars, unreadable books and numbered notebooks as small props, so counting them reads on screen.
  - **Townsfolk:** the building manager, the glassy-eyed neighbor and her dog, two or three tenants, Walt's grown child on the phone or in person, plus 1–2 more the case file needs. About 1 in 3 with one weird trait (Art → Townsfolk and monsters). Map sprites and 64 × 64 portraits.
  - **Walt:** map sprite and portrait. How and when he appears is the case file's call.
  - **Monsters:** whatever the case file designs, following Art → Townsfolk and monsters, with battle art in the pattern Demo 5 part B sets.
  - **Palette accents:** sodium orange, bug-light purple, pool-tile teal, and the tent's warning stripes.
- **Candidates for the Shared Shelf** (flag for Keegan's OK first, Season readiness step 6): the two-story walkway and metal stairs, numbered doors, window AC unit, chain-link fence, the dry pool, the parking lot lines, the work light, and the striped tent if it reads as generic.
