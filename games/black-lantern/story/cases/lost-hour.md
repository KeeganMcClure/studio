## The truth (what is really going on)

- **What lives in the fog.** Above the Blue Ridge, in the spring fogs, something keeps a survey. The agency's old files call them **the Measurers**: tall, pale, patient things that come down out of a cold light to take living things off the ridge road for an hour, measure them, and put them back. They have done it to deer, bears, a school bus in 1971 (nobody noticed: everyone on it was asleep) and, every few springs, to a car. They are not cruel. They are thorough. Being thorough is worse.
- **The cold light.** What people see over the trees is the **Lamp**, the hole the Measurers come down through. It doesn't blink like a plane because it isn't one. It hangs where the fog is thickest and the road is highest: over the overlook at mile 31.
- **What happened to the Pruetts.** Ray (64) and Donna (62) left their daughter Jenna's wedding reception at the Grange hall in Elkton at ten. On the ridge road the car died at mile 31, in fog, with the radio still playing. A cold white light came down on the hood (the scorched ring). They were lifted, car and all, into the Lamp. Inside, an hour and then five more: they were laid on cold tables, opened and closed without a scar, sung at, counted, and made to answer questions in voices that were their own. Then they were put back in their driveway at 3:15 with a full tank (the Measurers fill whatever they empty; it's good manners to them) and the hours folded away.
- **The lump.** Behind Ray's left ear is a **tally**: a pearl-gray seed the size of a pea, folded out of Ray's own bone. It is how the Measurers find a specimen again. It hums when the fog comes down. It has a root. It is due to be collected at the next heavy fog, which the forecast says is two nights off: they take the tally back, and the tally does not come out alone.
- **The folded card.** Donna had Jenna's wedding card in her lap, the one with the check in it they never gave her. The Measurers folded it the way they fold everything they keep track of: into a shape with more inside than outside. It fell from the Lamp onto the overlook when they were put back. It will not unfold for anyone who tries to unfold it. It unfolds for someone who *remembers* it (Donna, under hypnosis, telling it what it was), or under Science plus Occult with the right fold order from the timeline. Inside, among the card's pieces and the check, is **a sheet that wasn't in the card**: the arc's page (below).
- **Donna's light.** Donna can't sleep without the lights on because, in the Lamp, it was dark except for the place they were working on. She remembers that much. She has not told Ray what she remembers about Ray.
- **The soft clock.** The couple are close to calling the county paper (the *Page Valley Courier*). Ray wants to; Donna doesn't. If they call, the cover cracks (Overstepping +1 step, public) and Jenna drives up, angry. Each block of time spent (a second drive, a long rest, a stakeout at the clinic) moves them a step closer. Three steps, and they call.
- **Rules (the internal logic).**
  1. The Measurers come only in fog and only to high places. Clear air, or low ground, and they can't reach you.
  2. A tally calls its Measurer. Wherever the tally goes on the collection night, the Lamp comes down there.
  3. Light that is not theirs hurts them. A lantern, a road flare, headlights: each makes them flinch and slows them. White light from their Lamp heals them.
  4. They keep what they measure. Anything they took (the hours, the card's shape, the tally) can be given back by someone who remembers it truly.
  5. They answer to counting. A Measurer that is counted out loud, correctly (Science or Occult, after the hypnosis memories), loses its place and must start over.

## The hard choice (about people who did nothing wrong)

Night 2. The tally calls the Lamp. Three options, each with a cost, none a trap:
1. **Bait.** Ray goes up the pasture with the team as the tally's carrier. The Lamp comes down on him; the team fights at his side. Fight as normal; if the boss lands its "Take" move on Ray (a chance each round in phase 2), Ray is lifted and lost (`worst` for Ray). Ray must agree (Persuade or Empathy; Donna begs him not to).
2. **Cut it out.** Dr. Rao (or a Medicine agent at −20 without him) removes the tally in the exam room on day 2. Ray lives but loses things: the hours of the wedding, his daughter's face in it, gone with the tally's root (Jenna's grief, Donna's). The tally, out, still calls: the team carries it up the pasture in a jar. The boss is weaker (no "Take" target, fights distracted: −1 to its attacks). Medicine roll: on a fail Ray has a stroke on the table (lives, costly).
3. **Give it to someone who chose it.** Donna, from memory2, asks for it: the Measurers "keep" what is given freely, and she wants to trade herself for him. Occult knows it can be moved (a willing carrier). Or an **agent** volunteers instead: the tally moves into the agent (Occult + Medicine). The agent carries it into the fight; they're the "Take" target; afterward they keep a lasting mark (a disorder roll, a hum in the fog every future case's foggy night, a Season 2 seed). Donna as carrier: she might be lost.

The endings react: who carried it, and whether they came home.
