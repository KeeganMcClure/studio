## The truth (what is really going on)

- **The collector.** **Osric Penhallow**, died in March at 88 in a house in Bastrop County with every window painted over. He kept things the way other men keep bees. His family sold the estate through a Round Rock auction house and asked never to be told who bought what. They know what he was. They don't say.
- **The buyer.** **Walt Okafor**, 71, Unit 6, Pecan Court Apartments. Retired school-bus mechanic. His wife **Grace** died fourteen months ago. He buys estate lots by the pallet and resells what he can on weekends; mostly it's just a reason to leave the apartment. He bid on lot 83 because it was cheap and because Grace's birthday is the 8th of March.
- **What came in the box.** Forty sealed specimen jars, a crate of books in no language anyone has catalogued, and a run of numbered ledgers, **1 to 90, with 83 missing**. Each jar holds a pale, folded thing in cloudy brine: an unhatched **Lodger**. Penhallow kept them cold. In a cool cellar they sleep forever.
- **What a Lodger is.** A blind larva with one huge milky eye that can't see. It senses two things: warmth and grief. It hatches in heat near a grieving person, and it **takes care of them back**: it hums, it presses close, it makes the empty side of the bed feel warm again. In return it feeds, slowly, and it nests. When it's grown it goes into the walls to pupate where the building is warmest, and it takes its person with it as the cocoon's lining. What comes out wants out of the building, and then wants the next lonely place.
- **What happened to Walt.** August, 104°, the AC in Unit 6 broke the day the boxes came. One jar warmed on the counter and hatched. Walt thought it was a sick animal. Then he thought it was Grace, a little. He fed it. He stopped answering his phone because it doesn't like the ringing. Three days later it was the size of a dog and the pest-control tech who came for "a smell" opened the wall behind Walt's fridge and quit on the spot. The neighbor's dog stopped barking at Unit 6 because it's afraid to.
- **Its brood.** A Lodger sheds **nits**: finger-long blind grubs that carry warmth back to the nest through the walls. They're the noises in the walls, the swarm enemy, and the reason the building's other jars matter: a nit will carry an unhatched jar's brine back to the nest, and every jar that reaches it heals it.
- **The missing ledger.** **Ledger 83** is Penhallow's own care manual: how to keep them asleep (cold, below 40°F), what wakes them (heat, grief, a name said kindly), and how to end one (cold to stop it, then fire). Walt took it out of the box on the first night and read it, and then hid it so nobody could take his Lodger away: **in the freezer, behind the ice trays, under the grandkid's drawing held on by a magnet**. Ledger 83 also carries the arc's page (below).
- **The night.** The case's tenting is real cover and real help: the gas doesn't hurt a Lodger, but clearing the tenants means nobody else gets taken. With the tenants gone and the building sealed, the Lodger stops hiding. Its pupation finishes at dawn, when the tent comes down. That's the soft clock.
- **Rules (the internal logic).**
  1. Lodgers wake in heat and sleep in cold. Under 40°F they go still; ice packed on a jar keeps it asleep.
  2. They find people by grief, not sight. A grieving agent (a Bond at 0, a lost agent in the party's past, a SAN under 40) draws them: roamers notice that agent first.
  3. A nit carrying brine heals the nest. Every jar counted and kept cold is one less heal.
  4. A Lodger won't let go of its person while that person still wants it. Walt has to be talked down (Empathy) before he can be pulled out safely (Medicine).
  5. Fire ends what cold has stopped. Fire alone just makes them angry.

### The boss: The Lodger

- **Base:** 60 HP, DEX 8, 55%, 1d6 (crush), all-target 1d3 "it hums" + 0/1d3 SAN, heals 4 each time a crawler reaches it (one per un-iced jar, up to 3). Tags unnatural, burns, lightshy. Weakness (Identify): **cold, then fire**: cold (an extinguisher, the ice, a fogger) stuns it one round and doubles the next fire damage.
- **Phase 1 (nesting):** it keeps Walt in its folds; attacks that miss it by 20+ hit Walt.
- **Phase 2 (at half): it breaks out of the pipes**, faster (DEX 12), gains a shield (half damage) unless its name is spoken (`read`).
- **Weakening choices:** `iced` all: no heals; `ledger`: Identify free; `walt` talked down: no "Gracie" heal (+8 HP once) and Walt can be pulled out; `read`: no shield. All taken: about 5–8 rounds. None: 8–12 (tools/boss-sim.js against the Gleaner's numbers).
