# SPOILERS: Personal motivation arcs, Season 1

**For Claude only.** The player chose (Nov 19, 2026) to have Claude write these without review. Never quote, summarize or hint at this file in chat, the Build Bible, pitches or Changelog lines. If asked, say it exists and decline unless the player clearly says to spoil it. Bible rules win on mechanics; this file holds story. Voice follows the Bible's Voice section.

## Shared rules

- **Who stars.** BOND1 = the agent's Bond with the higher score when beat 1 plays (tie: the first one named in the creator). BOND2 = the other. Lines use the Bond's name and relationship ("your sister, Dana"). If a beat's role doesn't fit BOND1 (a child can't have been at a 2009 drowning), use BOND2; if neither fits, the role goes to a non-Bond relative or old friend invented at build time, and any "broken Bond" ending becomes the arc's listed disorder instead.
- **A Bond already at 0** can't star; use the other. Both at 0: the arc runs with an invented stand-in and a bad ending gives the disorder.
- **Choices.** Beats 1 and 2 each end with two options, tagged here **Hold** and **Slip** (tags never shown). Slip always pays something right now (usually +1 SAN that night) so it's a real temptation.
- **Endings.** Hold + Hold = good ending (signature perk). Slip + Slip = bad ending. Mixed = good ending with a cost: BOND1 loses 1d4 (minimum 1).
- **Shield link / swap:** as the Bible says. Giving up Main at a Breaking Point plays the bad finale right then, in a 3-box version.
- **Staging:** beats are short home cutscenes (portrait + dialog box), 10–20 boxes each. Places are generic and get the agent's hometown name when built. Template agents get hand-made swaps of names and details at build time.
- **Perks** are listed with their exact effect. None needs an item.

## 1. Protect the innocent: "Jojo"

**Beat 1 (after case 1).** 11:40 at night. A kid is sitting on the agent's front steps in a Spider-Man hoodie: Jojo, 9, from two doors down. He's not crying. He's eating dry Froot Loops from the box.
- JOJO: Mom's boyfriend is being loud. I'm not allowed back till it's quiet.
- JOJO: You have cable? We had cable.
- Through the wall of the next house: a thump, then a TV turned up. Nothing shown.
- Choice: **Hold** "Call it in right." The agent calls the non-emergency line and CPS, and sits with Jojo till a patrol car comes. Jojo looks at the agent like a traitor. **Slip** "Handle it yourself." The agent walks over. Narration: Two minutes. The boyfriend's truck leaves with one headlight. (+1 SAN; overstepping +1 step in the next case.)

**Beat 2 (after case 3).** 2 a.m. knock. Jojo, with a bruise he explains too fast ("I fell off the monkey bars. At night.").
- Hold path lead-in: his grandma is fighting for custody; the boyfriend is back on weekends.
- Slip path lead-in: the boyfriend filed an assault complaint; Jojo stopped coming outside.
- JOJO: Can I sleep on your couch? I won't eat anything. Except the Froot Loops.
- BOND1 is on the phone or in the room: "You can't adopt every stray on the block."
- Choice: **Hold** "Do it the long way." Drive Jojo to his grandma at 2 a.m., wake a caseworker, sign statements. **Slip** "Let him stay. Tell no one." (+1 SAN; he sleeps, finally.)

**Finale (after case 5).**
- Good: Grandma's porch, a Saturday. Jojo hands over a crayon drawing: the agent with a giant flashlight, monsters running. "That's you. You're bigger in it." Narration: You are. You'll keep it in your wallet till the crayon wears off.
- Bad: The house two doors down has a For Sale sign. Nobody will say where Jojo went. Narration: You check the lock three times before bed. Then four.
- **Perk, Not on my watch:** once per fight, when an ally would be Downed, this agent takes the hit instead at half damage.
- **Bad ending:** disorder, Hypervigilance (can't take a full rest unless the agent spends a turn "checking the doors"; any rest restores half).

## 2. Find the truth: "Danny Ruiz"

**Beat 1.** A Facebook memory: "15 years ago today we lost Danny." Danny Ruiz, a friend from high school, drowned in a stock pond in 2009. Ruled an accident. BOND1 changes the subject too fast.
- BOND1: He was drunk. Everybody was drunk. That's the whole story.
- Narration: Nobody says "that's the whole story" about a whole story.
- Choice: **Hold** "Ask his mother first." Mrs. Ruiz says yes over the phone and then cries for a while, politely. **Slip** "Pull the file." The agent uses agency access to pull the sealed sheriff's report. (+1 SAN; real lead now: page 4 is missing.)

**Beat 2.** The truth is small and human. Danny wasn't alone at the pond. A second set of tracks. A borrowed truck. The person who drove away was BOND1.
- BOND1: He said he was fine. He said "go, I'm fine." I was seventeen.
- BOND1: I've thought about it every day. You want to know a secret? Every day is not an exaggeration.
- Choice: **Hold** "Ask BOND1 to tell her." The agent says they'll go with them. **Slip** "Tell her yourself." The agent calls Mrs. Ruiz that night. (+1 SAN; the relief of saying it.)

**Finale.**
- Good: The pond, early morning. BOND1 and Mrs. Ruiz sitting on a tailgate, not talking. She hands BOND1 a thermos. Narration: That's not forgiveness. It's coffee. It's a start.
- Bad: BOND1's number is disconnected. A postcard from two states away: "You could have let me."
- **Perk, Loose thread:** once per case, a wrong deduction costs nothing (no SAN, time or ending penalty), and the agent may try again.
- **Bad ending:** broken Bond (BOND1 to 0).

## 3. Family: "Pell Street"

(If neither Bond is family, a parent who isn't a Bond carries this arc and the bad ending is the disorder.)

**Beat 1.** The agent's mother (or father, or whoever raised them) calls. The childhood house on Pell Street is sold. Closing is in three weeks. Come help pack.
- MOM: I kept your lighters. In a box. Somewhere. Don't ask where.
- Narration: She says it like a dare. Your room's still a ten-year-old's room. It always will be.
- Choice: **Hold** "Go home." The agent gives up a weekend BOND1 had planned. **Slip** "Send money for movers." (+1 SAN; a weekend with BOND1 that feels like a vacation.)

**Beat 2.** In the attic, under the Christmas boxes: a shoebox of letters to a P.O. box, and a photo of a toddler in a car seat. Birth date two years before the agent's. On the back: "Our Annie. We're sorry."
- Mom's line if the agent went home: I was going to burn those. I kept not doing it.
- If Slip in beat 1, the agent finds it in the box the movers shipped, alone.
- Choice: **Hold** "Find Annie." The agent asks the parent's blessing and starts looking. **Slip** "Burn it. Keep the peace." The box goes in the fire pit. (+1 SAN; Mom hugs you and means it.)

**Finale.**
- Good: A diner booth. A woman with the agent's exact nose. She's brought her own kids' school photos and lays them out like a hand of cards. ANNIE: So. You're the one they kept. Narration: She laughs first. That's how you know it'll be all right.
- Bad: The last box comes out of Pell Street. In it, the Christmas ornaments, a dead phone charger, and nothing else. Narration: Some families keep secrets. Yours kept you.
- **Perk, Call home:** once per case, from the map menu, the agent phones a Bond: clears Shaken, +1d4 WP, and that Bond gains +1.
- **Bad ending:** broken Bond (the family Bond to 0); if the parent isn't a Bond, disorder, Insomnia (rests restore 1 less HP and WP, minimum 1).

## 4. Faith: "The Tent on Route 9"

(Arc touch, see claude/SPOILERS-agency-arc.md: the deacon is the Almoner. First sighting follows the cameo rule: lead agent SAN 0/1. Never named here or anywhere the player can see.)

**Beat 1.** BOND1 has been sick for a year: pain no doctor can name. A flyer on the fridge: HEALING REVIVAL, ALL WELCOME, ROUTE 9, NO COLLECTION TAKEN. That last line is underlined twice.
- BOND1: No collection. When's the last time church didn't pass a plate?
- Narration: Whatever you believe, or used to, it gets quiet in you when you read that line.
- Choice: **Hold** "Go with them." The agent stands in the back of the tent. At the far edge of the crowd, a tall man in an old deacon's coat with a ledger under his arm. Eyes closed. Turned toward you anyway. (cameo; SAN 0/1) **Slip** "It's a con. Stay home." BOND1 stays home, angry. (+1 SAN; you're sure. Tonight.)

**Beat 2.** BOND1 is better. Not cured; better. They got a visit at home. A courteous man, a paper, a pen.
- BOND1: He said I only have to sign when I'm ready. He said there's no hurry. Isn't that the kindest thing?
- The agent finds the paper on the counter. Narration: The line for a signature is already a little worn. Like a lot of thumbs have rested there.
- Choice: **Hold** "Tear it up. Sit with them." The agent and BOND1 pray, or don't, at the kitchen table until sunrise. BOND1's pain comes back by morning. **Slip** "Sign it yourself, so they don't have to." (+1 SAN; BOND1 sleeps without pain for the first time in a year. Secret flag: this agent is an account. Season 2 consequence. Never shown.)

**Finale.**
- Good: Route 9. Where the tent was, a square of yellow grass. BOND1 holds the agent's hand and says the pain's bad today, and that's fine. Narration: Nobody passed a plate. Nobody had to.
- Bad: The agent wakes up with the pen in their hand. They don't own a pen like that.
- **Perk, Steady faith:** horror-on-sight SAN losses for this agent are reduced by 1 (minimum 0). Doesn't count as a halving.
- **Bad ending:** disorder, Scrupulosity (once per case, at the first unnatural clue, the agent must pass a POW×5 roll or lose 1 WP to "check it again").

## 5. Revenge: "Car Wash"

**Beat 1.** The agent pulls into a car wash. The guy drying windshields is Curtis Pyle. Eleven years ago, Curtis ran a red light drunk and killed BOND2's (or the agent's) little brother, Tyler. He served 18 months. He has a name tag now. CURTIS.
- CURTIS: Want the tire shine? It's two bucks but I'll do it for free.
- Narration: He doesn't know you. That's the worst part. You've known him every day.
- Choice: **Hold** "Drive away." **Slip** "Follow him home." (+1 SAN; you know where he sleeps now, and that's something.)

**Beat 2.** Curtis is sober. Nine years. He coaches T-ball on Saturdays. His kid plays second base and picks flowers in the outfield.
- If Slip: Curtis noticed the car. He leaves a note on the windshield: "I know who you are. I'm sorry. I'm not going anywhere."
- BOND1: You want him dead or you want him sorry? Because he's already sorry.
- Choice: **Hold** "Say it to his face." In daylight, on the bleachers, then leave. **Slip** "Let him see you. Every Saturday." (+1 SAN; he flinches every time.)

**Finale.**
- Good: The cemetery. The agent tells Tyler's headstone about the T-ball kid picking flowers. Narration: You put the grudge down. It's heavier than you thought. Your arms remember.
- Bad: Narration only, flat: Curtis didn't coach that Saturday. Or the next. The league sent a card. Then black, then the agent washing their hands at the sink for a long time.
- **Perk, Cold head:** this agent can't be Shaken by human enemies, and gets +20% to hit any enemy that Downed an ally this fight.
- **Bad ending:** disorder, Rage (when an ally is Downed, the agent's next turn must be an attack on the nearest enemy, no specials).

## 6. Duty: "The Hearing"

**Beat 1.** A call from the agent's old life: Ruth Okafor, a former partner from the profession the agent had before Black Lantern (a unit, a precinct, an ER, a newsroom). A review board is reopening "the Garza thing." Ruth wants the agent to back her version.
- RUTH: Remember it how I remember it. That's all I'm asking. You owe me two ribs and a wedding toast.
- Narration: You don't remember it how she remembers it. You remember it how it happened.
- Choice: **Hold** "I'll tell it straight." **Slip** "I've got your back." (+1 SAN; old loyalty feels like a warm coat.)

**Beat 2.** The hearing is next week. Big Hat calls on a Sunday.
- BIG HAT: You were never at that job. You've got no last name in that room. You go in there and tell it straight, a lawyer pulls your file, and your file is a door I can't have open.
- BIG HAT: Don't make it weird.
- Choice: **Hold** "Testify anyway." The agent tells the truth and keeps the agency out of it, barely. (Next case starts with overstepping +1.) **Slip** "Stay home, like he says." (+1 SAN; Big Hat sends a box of crullers. No note.)

**Finale.**
- Good: Ruth, outside the courthouse, smoking. RUTH: You sold me out. RUTH: ...Good. Somebody had to. Narration: She owes you two ribs now. You'll collect.
- Bad: The board rules in Ruth's favor. The Garza family is on the news. BOND1 watches it and doesn't look at you.
- **Perk, Badge:** once per case, one roll against police, officials or agency staff automatically succeeds.
- **Bad ending:** broken Bond (BOND1 to 0).

## 7. Curiosity: "3:12"

**Beat 1.** The old clock radio in the agent's kitchen, unplugged for years, plays at 3:12 a.m. A numbers station. The voice reading the numbers is BOND1's.
- RADIO: Four. Eleven. Six. Six. Two. Four. Eleven.
- Narration: BOND1 is asleep across town. You checked. You checked twice.
- Choice: **Hold** "Write it down, turn it off." **Slip** "Talk back." The agent says "Who is this?" into the radio. The numbers stop. Breathing. Then they start again, a little faster. (+1 SAN; the thrill is a drug.)

**Beat 2.** The numbers were dates and house numbers. Each one: a small fire, a gas leak, a car in a lake, a week later. The newest set is BOND1's address and tomorrow.
- Narration: You could be wrong. You've never been wrong.
- Choice: **Hold** "Get BOND1 out tonight." The agent drives BOND1 to a motel, no explanation; BOND1 is furious. **Slip** "Go alone and watch at 3:12." (+1 SAN; you see it happen: nothing happens. Then the porch light at BOND1's goes out by itself.)

**Finale.**
- Good: 3:12. The radio plays one last broadcast in BOND1's voice: "Thank you for listening." Then a song the agent's grandmother used to hum. Then nothing, forever.
- Bad: The radio is in the trash. It plays from the trash. The agent starts writing numbers in the margins of case files without noticing.
- **Perk, Seen it before:** the first time this agent fights each enemy type, they learn its weakness for free (as Identify, no roll).
- **Bad ending:** disorder, Obsession (once per case, at the first hotspot, the lead must spend extra time there if this agent is in the party: day turns toward night one step sooner).

## 8. Money: "Nine Thousand"

**Beat 1.** BOND1 needs money: an emergency vet bill, back rent or a bail bond, whichever fits them. Nine thousand dollars. The agent's account has four hundred and twelve.
- BOND1: I'll pay you back. I'll pay you back with interest. I'll pay you back with my blood.
- Narration: The case file in your bag has an ugly little statue in it. A man online offered six figures for "anything from the Lantern program." He spelled it right.
- Choice: **Hold** "Do it the hard way." A second job, the car sold, a payment plan. **Slip** "Sell the statue." (+1 SAN; the money is real. Secret: counts as one hidden item against the finale, per the agency arc file. Never shown.)

**Beat 2.** The collector writes again. Hold path: he heard the agent has access, and offers more. Slip path: he wants more, and he knows the agent's home address.
- COLLECTOR (text): Not threatening. Just a fan. Big fan. Of your front door.
- Choice: **Hold** "Tell Big Hat." BIG HAT: Put a bow on this. (The collector vanishes from the internet by morning.) **Slip** "Sell him one more thing." (+1 SAN; the bill's paid off. BOND1 cries with relief.)

**Finale.**
- Good: A kitchen table, a laptop, a spreadsheet called BROKE BUT ALIVE. BOND1 and the agent eating cereal for dinner and laughing. Narration: It isn't paid off. It isn't close. You're not alone with it.
- Bad: BOND1 finds the collector's texts. They don't yell. They put the money on the counter in an envelope, every dollar, and leave.
- **Perk, Expense account:** once per case, from the map menu, the agent gets one Common item from the Item list.
- **Bad ending:** broken Bond (BOND1 to 0).

## 9. Redemption: "Carla's"

**Beat 1.** A letter at the agent's door, no stamp. It's from Carla Dunmore. Years ago the agent left Carla behind on the worst night of both their lives (the fire at the Dunmore place; the agent got out a window and didn't go back). Carla has burn scars and a diner now.
- LETTER: I know where you work. Not that work. The other one. Come eat. I won't spit in it.
- Choice: **Hold** "Go to the diner." **Slip** "Don't. Change the locks." (+1 SAN; relief, like ice on a burn.)

**Beat 2.** Carla doesn't want an apology. Her teenage son, Eli, has started asking about the fire, and Carla's told him a kinder story. She wants the agent to tell him the true one. With BOND1 there, because "you need a witness that you said it."
- CARLA: I'm not forgiving you. I'm just tired of carrying both halves.
- Choice: **Hold** "Tell Eli the truth." All of it: the window, the not going back. **Slip** "Tell the better version." Smoke, confusion, nobody's fault. (+1 SAN; Eli shakes your hand.)

**Finale.**
- Good: Carla's diner, a Tuesday. She refills the agent's coffee without asking. Eli is doing homework in a booth. CARLA: Pie's on the house. Coffee you're paying for. Narration: That's the closest thing to grace you'll get in this life. It's enough.
- Bad: Eli finds the fire report online. BOND1 was there for the better version. Narration: Now two people know what you are. One of them sleeps next to you. Or used to.
- **Perk, Second chance:** once per case, reroll a failed SAN roll.
- **Bad ending:** broken Bond (BOND1 to 0) if BOND1 was the witness; otherwise disorder, Depression (WP maximum −2 until the agent's next Personal Motivation home scene succeeds).
