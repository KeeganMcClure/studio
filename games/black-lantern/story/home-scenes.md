# Black Lantern: home scenes (season 1)

Private authoring doc for spec 008 (Systems → Home scenes). Never copy this file into the public studio repo; only the lines go into `games/black-lantern/src/home.js` (the `HT` text block).

Last updated 2026-11-23 for spec 008 part C (enough scenes for a season), then home phase part D (Close goes to 7 per Bond type). Existing scenes kept, in order; new ones follow each pool. Lines are grouped as they play, one box per bullet. `{A}` the agent, `{B}` the Bond, `{T}` a Therapist in the party; pronouns are always tokens (`{B.they}`, `{B.their}`, `{B:doesn't|don't}`), never a fixed he or she.

## Fulfill Responsibilities

### Partner

#### Close

1.
   - The kitchen smells like garlic and somebody trying.
   - **BOND:** You're home. You smell like a campfire and a hospital.
   - **AGENT:** Long week.
   - **BOND:** Don't want to know. Sit. I made the thing.
   - The thing is lasagna. It's still frozen in the middle. Nobody mentions it.

2.
   - {B} is asleep on the couch. The TV is asking if anyone's still watching.
   - **BOND:** Mm. You're back. Did you eat?
   - **AGENT:** Gas station burrito.
   - **BOND:** That's not eating. That's a dare.
   - {B} makes you a grilled cheese at midnight, half asleep, and burns one side.
   - You eat the burnt side. That's the deal.

3.
   - A list on the fridge: GUTTERS. DISPOSAL. CALL YOUR MOTHER.
   - **BOND:** Pick one. I did the other two while you were gone.
   - **AGENT:** Which two?
   - **BOND:** Wouldn't you like to know.
   - You clean the gutters. Something up there made a nest out of your old work shirt.
   - You leave it. It worked hard for that.

4.
   - {B} is in the bathtub with the door open, reading a library book that's eleven weeks overdue.
   - **BOND:** Don't look at me. I'm hiding from the fines.
   - **AGENT:** How much?
   - **BOND:** Four dollars and my good name.
   - You sit on the bathmat and read {B} the dirty parts out loud. There aren't any. You make some up.

5.
   - The kitchen radio only gets one station. On Sundays it's polka.
   - **BOND:** Dance with me. That's an order.
   - **AGENT:** I don't know how to polka.
   - **BOND:** Nobody does. That's the secret. You bounce until somebody gets hurt.
   - You bounce. {B} steps on your foot twice.
   - On purpose, the second time.

6.
   - {B} cut {B.their} own hair while you were gone. It's a choice.
   - **BOND:** Say one word about the back.
   - **AGENT:** I wasn't going to.
   - **BOND:** You were. You had the face.
   - You fix the back with the kitchen scissors. It's worse now. You both agree it's worse.
   - {B} wears it to the grocery store anyway, holding your hand the whole time, like you're both in on it.

7.
   - 3 a.m. The neighbor's car alarm is working through its whole songbook.
   - **BOND:** Go out there and reason with it.
   - **AGENT:** It's a car.
   - **BOND:** You reason with my mother. Same thing.
   - {B} names every sound it makes. The siren is Linda. The one that goes whoop is Duane.
   - You fall asleep holding hands, somewhere around Duane.

#### Strained

1.
   - {B} is on the couch with the TV on mute.
   - **BOND:** Your phone was off for six days.
   - **AGENT:** Work.
   - **BOND:** Work. Right. Pest control.
   - You eat on opposite ends of the couch. A cooking show nobody's watching wins.

2.
   - {B} is doing the dishes. Loudly.
   - **BOND:** The car smells like wet dog and a fire. We don't have a dog.
   - **AGENT:** Work thing.
   - **BOND:** Your work things have a smell now. That's new.
   - You dry. {B} washes. Nobody says anything until the forks.

3.
   - **BOND:** Your mother called here. Twice. I said you were on a fishing trip.
   - **AGENT:** I don't fish.
   - **BOND:** I know. She knows. Everybody knows but the fish.
   - Dinner is takeout, eaten out of the containers.
   - Nobody gets plates down. Plates would mean staying.

#### Fraying

1.
   - There's a suitcase by the door. It isn't yours.
   - **BOND:** I'm not leaving. I just like knowing it's packed.
   - **AGENT:** That's insane.
   - **BOND:** You came home with somebody else's blood in your hair. Twice, babe.
   - You sleep on the couch. The suitcase watches you all night.

2.
   - You come in the back way. {B} is at the kitchen table in the dark.
   - **BOND:** I heard the truck an hour ago. You sat out there an hour.
   - **AGENT:** I was finishing a call.
   - **BOND:** Your phone was on the counter. It's still on the counter.
   - {B} goes to bed. You sit in the dark kitchen until the fridge stops humming. Then a while after.

3.
   - {B} has started sleeping with the hall light on.
   - **BOND:** You talk in your sleep now. Did you know that?
   - **AGENT:** What do I say?
   - **BOND:** Numbers. And "don't open it." Over and over. Real polite.
   - You don't sleep. You lie there being polite to nobody.

#### Broken (the last scene)

1.
   - The house is quieter than a house should be.
   - The good knives are gone. The dog is gone. The suitcase is gone.
   - On the fridge, under the pizza magnet: "Don't call. I mean it this time."
   - You don't call. You'll think about that magnet a lot, later.

### Kid

#### Close

1.
   - {B} has been at the front window since lunch.
   - **BOND:** Did you catch any bad guys?
   - **AGENT:** A couple.
   - **BOND:** Were they REAL bad or regular bad?
   - **AGENT:** Regular. Mostly.
   - You get dragged out back to meet a worm named Kevin. Kevin is the most important thing that happened all week.

2.
   - {B} has built a fort out of every couch cushion in the house.
   - **BOND:** Password.
   - **AGENT:** Uh. Pizza?
   - **BOND:** That was last week's. But okay.
   - Inside: a flashlight, three crackers, and a crayon monster taped to the wall.
   - **BOND:** That's so the real ones think it's taken.

3.
   - {B} lost a tooth while you were gone and saved it in a sandwich bag to show you.
   - **BOND:** The tooth fairy gave me a dollar. A WHOLE dollar.
   - **AGENT:** Inflation.
   - **BOND:** What's inflation?
   - You explain it with a pile of cereal. By the end, {B} has eaten the economy.

4.
   - {B} learned to whistle while you were gone. {B} would like you to know that. Constantly.
   - **BOND:** Listen. LISTEN.
   - **AGENT:** I'm listening, bud.
   - It's one note. It's the same note for forty minutes.
   - At dinner {B} whistles at the peas. You laugh milk out your nose. Nobody has ever been prouder.

5.
   - The goldfish died Tuesday. {B} waited for you to do the funeral.
   - **BOND:** You have to say words. Like on TV.
   - **AGENT:** Uh. Bubbles was a good fish.
   - **BOND:** It was DOCTOR Bubbles.
   - You flush Doctor Bubbles with full honors. {B} salutes the toilet.
   - The words come easy. You've had practice lately.

6.
   - The training wheels are off. {B} wants you to see. Right now. In socks.
   - **BOND:** Don't let go. Okay, let go. No, hold on. LET GO.
   - **AGENT:** I let go a block ago.
   - {B} looks back, sees you way behind, and rides straight into the Hendersons' hedge.
   - {B} comes out with leaves in {B.their} hair, yelling "AGAIN."

7.
   - {B} made you a trophy. A toilet paper tube, most of a roll of tinfoil, and a lot of glue.
   - **BOND:** Read the bottom.
   - It says BEST GROWED-UP IN THE WHOLE STREET.
   - **AGENT:** Just the street?
   - **BOND:** I don't know everybody.
   - It goes on the dash of the truck. It's still there, later, when nothing else is.

#### Strained

1.
   - {B} doesn't look up from the tablet.
   - **AGENT:** Hey, bud. I'm back.
   - **BOND:** Okay.
   - You missed the recital. There's a video. {B} says you don't have to watch it.
   - You watch it four times in the truck.

2.
   - {B} is at the kitchen table doing homework you don't understand.
   - **AGENT:** Need help?
   - **BOND:** You weren't here for the part where they explained it.
   - Fair. You sit there anyway, pretending to read the fractions.
   - At bedtime {B} says "night" to the doorframe near you. Close enough.

3.
   - The science fair was Thursday. {B}'s volcano got an honorable mention.
   - **BOND:** Everybody gets honorable mention.
   - **AGENT:** Show me how it goes off.
   - A shrug. Then a long time in the yard, getting the baking soda just right.
   - It foams all over the grass. {B} almost smiles at you. Almost counts.

#### Fraying

1.
   - {B}'s door has a new sign. KEEP OUT. The OUT is underlined twice.
   - **AGENT:** Brought you a snow globe. It's got a little lighthouse in it.
   - A long quiet.
   - **BOND:** Why do you always smell like that when you come back?
   - You don't have an answer. You leave the snow globe on the carpet.

2.
   - {B} has started checking the locks. Every door. Twice.
   - **AGENT:** Who taught you that?
   - **BOND:** You did. You do it every time you come home.
   - You didn't know you did that.
   - You stand in the hall with your hand on the deadbolt for a long time.

3.
   - {B}'s teacher sent a drawing home. Your house, with a black car out front.
   - **BOND:** That's the man who sits outside when you're gone.
   - **AGENT:** What man?
   - **BOND:** He waves. I don't wave back. You said don't.
   - You never said that. You check the street. Nobody.
   - You check it again at three.

#### Broken (the last scene)

1.
   - The family lawyer has a fern and a bowl of mints nobody eats.
   - "Supervised visits," she says. "Considering the... gaps."
   - The drawing {B} made of you is still in your wallet. You're tall in it. You're smiling.
   - You have a gun for a hand.

### Parent

#### Close

1.
   - {B}'s house still has the plastic on the good couch.
   - **BOND:** You're thin. Are they feeding you at that job?
   - **AGENT:** There's a donut shop.
   - **BOND:** A donut is not a meal. Sit. I'll heat up the roast.
   - There's always a roast. You've never once seen it start out raw.

2.
   - {B} has the photo albums out on the kitchen table. Again.
   - **BOND:** Look at that haircut. I let you leave the house like that.
   - **AGENT:** You cut it.
   - **BOND:** I did not. Your aunt did. With the good scissors.

3.

   - **BOND:** Take it to the hardware store. Get me the thing.
   - **AGENT:** What thing?
   - **BOND:** You'll know it when you see it.

   - {B} needed the second one. You're still a good kid.

4.
   - {B} is on the porch shelling peas into a bowl. Same bowl since you were six.
   - **BOND:** Sit. Make yourself useful. You remember how?
   - **AGENT:** Pop the end. Thumb down the seam.
   - **BOND:** Look at that. Something stuck.
   - You shell peas until the light goes. {B} tells you who died at church. It's a long list.

5.
   - {B} got a new phone. It's been yelling the weather at {B.them} since Easter.
   - **BOND:** Make it stop talking. I didn't ask it anything.
   - **AGENT:** You said "hey" to the cat.
   - **BOND:** I'm allowed to say hey to my own cat.

   - The card goes in the drawer with forty other index cards. Every one is your handwriting.

6.
   - {B} is making biscuits and won't write the recipe down. Never has.
   - **BOND:** You watch. That's how you learn. That's how I learned.
   - **AGENT:** How much flour?
   - **BOND:** Till it looks right.
   - They come out of the oven flat and perfect.
   - {B} hands you the first one off the pan. Too hot to hold. You hold it.

7.
   - **BOND:** Drive me out to see your grandfather. Bring the clippers.
   - **BOND:** He hated tall grass. He hated everything a little. That's how you knew he liked you.
   - **AGENT:** He liked me?
   - **BOND:** He hated you a normal amount.
   - You trim around the stone on your knees. {B} reads Grandpa the obituaries out loud, the good ones.
   - On the way out, {B} pats the stone twice, the way {B.they} used to pat your head.

2.

1.

2.

3.

#### Broken (the last scene)

1.
