# Life After survivor builder

Part of the Life After bible (`bible.md`, Your survivor). Set Nov 21 from Keegan's review of the survivor draft. Companions use the same skills, traits and looks parts. Exact numbers are tuned in playtests.

## Skills

8 skills, each from 0 to 5. Every job gives 5 skill points, with a 3 in its main skill.

**Your own tweak:** after you pick a job, you get 3 free points to put in any skills. No skill can start above 3.

| Skill | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Melee | Hit chance and damage with bats, blades and tools |
| Guns | Aim with guns and the bow; reloading faster |
| Sneak | Smaller noise ring when moving; harder to spot |
| Medicine | Healing more from each item; treating wounds and bites |
| Fixing | Locks, crafting, repairs, starting dead cars |
| Outdoors | Foraging, natural remedies, hunting, fishing, farming |
| Cooking | Meals fill more and lift SAN; makes spoiled or raw food safe |
| Talk | Trading, calming people, talking a fight down, bond with companions |

**Getting better:** every use counts toward the next level, and each level takes more uses than the last. There's no XP screen: your notebook says it in a line, like "You're getting better with a bat."

### Abilities

Each skill unlocks one ability at level 3 and a bigger one at level 5. Your notebook tells you when you get one.

| Skill | At 3 | At 5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melee | **Shove:** push a zombie back a tile, quietly | **Sweep:** one swing hits two zombies next to you |
| Guns | **Steady shot:** spend a turn aiming for a much better hit chance | **Headshot:** an aimed shot always kills a zombie |
| Sneak | **Soft steps:** walking is as quiet as sneaking | **Vanish:** break line of sight and enemies lose you at once |
| Medicine | **Field care:** treat deep cuts and breaks with rags, no kit needed | **Clean the bite:** a bite treated within the hour may not turn |
| Fixing | **Hotwire:** start a car without keys. It takes 30 minutes, it's loud, and it can set off the alarm. The car still needs gas, a battery and repairs | **Jury-rig:** fix a weapon to full, and craft big things without a workbench |
| Outdoors | **Tracker:** see zombie, people and animal tracks on the map | **Read the sky:** weather warnings as sure as a radio's, and you can forage in winter |
| Cooking | **Stretch it:** one meal feeds two people | **Comfort food:** a cooked dinner lifts everyone's SAN |
| Talk | **Read people:** see a stranger's mood before you speak | **Silver tongue:** talk down raiders even mid-fight, and sometimes win them over |

## Traits

Traits work on points, in one good list and one bad list (Keegan, Nov 21):

- Each good trait costs points, and each bad trait gives points.
- Your good points can't add up to more than your bad points, so any good trait means taking at least one bad one.
- Up to 3 good traits. You can't take two opposites (Light Sleeper and Heavy Sleeper, Strong Back and Bad Back).
- Companions get a balanced set the same way.

**Good traits (cost)**

- **Light Sleeper (2):** you always wake before anything reaches you.
- **Strong Back (2):** 2 extra pocket slots.
- **Steady Hands (3):** locks, searching and crafting take half the time.
- **Iron Gut (1):** raw meat, wild berries and spoiled food never make you sick.
- **Brave (3):** fear and gore cost half the SAN.
- **Calm (2):** at zero SAN you only freeze, never run, and SAN comes back faster at rest.
- **Kind (1):** people trust you faster, but killing people costs more SAN.
- **Quick Learner (3):** skills grow faster.
- **Night Owl (2):** you see 2 tiles farther at night.
- **Green Thumb (1):** crops grow a day faster.

**Bad traits (give)**

- **Smoker (1):** starts hooked on cigarettes. Without one each day, SAN slips.
- **Greedy (1):** you trade a little better, but companions bond with you slower.
- **Weak Stomach (1):** bad food makes you sick for longer.
- **Nearsighted (2):** you see 2 tiles less, and guns are harder to aim.
- **Hothead (2):** talking a fight down and trading go worse, and an insult can start a fight.
- **Jumpy (2):** surprises and dark nights cost extra SAN.
- **Stubborn (2):** skills grow slower, and companions' advice is easier to ignore by accident.
- **Heavy Sleeper (2):** noises rarely wake you.
- **Bad Back (2):** 2 fewer pocket slots.
- **Bad Knee (3):** fleeing gives you 1 fewer step, and stairs and fences take an extra turn.

## Wants

Pick one, or leave it random. Rumors can tie into it, and it gives a long run a reason to travel.

- **Find family:** someone you lost is out there, and some rumors are about them.
- **Get home:** a far-off town the roads may lead to.
- **Find the cure:** chase rumors of a lab or depot.
- **Make something last:** a hideout that feeds other people.
- **See the ocean:** a long, quiet hope.
- **Settle a score:** someone did something before the end, and they may still be alive.
- **Keep a promise:** carry something to someone.
- **Find out what happened:** the world's history, from notes and places.
- **Get clean:** stay off a vice you used to lean on. Each clean week lifts SAN, and a slip costs it.
- **Find the dog:** your dog ran off when it started. Sightings come up in rumors, and a dog that finds you is a companion that never argues.
- **Reach the safe zone:** the radio keeps naming a place. It may be real.
- **Make amends:** you did something before the end. Helping other survivors slowly settles it.

## Looks parts

- **Body type:** 3 (slim, average, heavy).
- **Skin tone:** 6.
- **Face:** 6.
- **Hair:** 8 styles (including bald) and 6 colors.
- **Clothes:** each job has its own outfit, in 4 color sets.
- **Age:** 3 ranges (young adult, middle-aged, older). Older survivors start with 1 more in their job skill but tire a little faster (Keegan, Nov 21).
