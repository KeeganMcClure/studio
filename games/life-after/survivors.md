# Life After other survivors and trade

Part of the Life After bible (`bible.md`, Other survivors and trade). Set Nov 23 from Keegan's review of the survivors and trade draft. Exact numbers are tuned in playtests.

## Group types

| Group | Size | Starting mood | What they want | Where you meet them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Loner | 1 | Wary | Food, to be left alone | Anywhere; hiding in houses |
| Family | 2 to 5, with kids | Wary to friendly | Medicine, a safe place | Homes, the church |
| Traders | 2 to 4 | Friendly | A fair trade, safe roads | The truck stop, the highway |
| Survivor camp | 6 to 15 | Wary | People who pull their weight | The school, the farm co-op |
| Raider gang | 3 to 8 | Hostile | Your stuff, your hideout | The motel, the gas station, roads |
| Holdouts | 2 to 6, armed | Hostile. They only talk if you come unarmed, bring something they need, or someone they trust speaks for you | Their turf left alone | A fortified store or farm |

Each group is generated with a leader (name, traits, a want), a mood, and a home on the map. The mood words are Hostile, Wary, Friendly and Allied.

**Traders** come in two kinds: traveling traders follow a route, and traders who stay put have more goods.

## What they remember

Every group keeps a hidden opinion of you, from -100 to +100, which sets its mood word. These change it:

| You did | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Traded fairly | +5 |
| Gave food or medicine when they needed it | +15 |
| Saved one of them | +25 |
| Kept a deal | +10 |
| Took a hard trade | -5 |
| Stole from them | -25 (if they find out) |
| Spared one of them in a fight | +10 (and they talk about it) |
| Killed one of them | -40 |
| Broke a deal | -30 |

- **Word spreads:** groups that trade with each other share news, so a killing on one side of town can sour traders on the other.
- **Raider gangs** keep their own grudge (see `bible.md`, Rumors and late threats). Sparing a raider may calm a gang, or it may let them find your hideout.
- **Allied** groups trade at the best rates and warn you about raiders. Only at the very top (opinion +80 or more), and only if they're close by, do they send help in an attack.

## Barter values

No money. Each item has a base value in points; a trade works when both sides' points match. These change the value:

- **Need:** a group short on something values it at double.
- **Season:** food is worth 1.5x in winter; fresh food is worth half in summer.
- **Trust:** Wary groups ask for 25% more; Allied groups give 25% better.
- **Talk skill:** each level shifts the deal about 5% your way.

| --- | --- |
| Antibiotics | 40 |
| The cure | Can't be bought |
| First aid kit | 25 |
| Box of rounds | 15 to 20 |

## Meeting a group

- You see them before they see you if you're sneaking. You can watch, walk up openly, call out, or avoid them.
- First talk shows their mood word and one thing they want.
- Choices: trade, ask for news (rumors), offer help, ask to join (a loner may join you), threaten, or leave.
- A loner or a family member can become a companion or a settler once they trust you.

## Joining raiders

An option for an "evil" run (Keegan, Nov 23).

- **The offer:** a gang may offer you a place if you've been useful to them (traded guns, helped a raid) or if they fear you (many kills, a strong hideout).
- **What you get:** their protection, a share of every raid, and no more raids on your own hideout from that gang.
- **What it costs:** you raid survivor groups and camps with them, so the rest of the world turns Hostile. Kind companions leave, and killing people costs SAN until you harden.
- **The danger:** the leader tests you with hard jobs, and may turn on you if you grow too strong. You can walk away, but the gang will hunt you.
