# Life After shelter

Part of the Life After bible (`bible.md`, Shelter). Set Nov 23 from Keegan's review of the shelter draft. It builds on the secret hideout rules (`bible.md`, The map, Rumors and late threats). Exact numbers are tuned in playtests.

## Claiming a hideout

- Any building with a door that closes can be claimed. Clear it of zombies, then choose Claim.
- Buildings differ: more doors and windows mean more to board up; more rooms mean more space to build.
- **More than one:** you can hold a main hideout plus safe houses. Each one can be found and raided on its own, and storage stays where you left it.

| Building | Good | Bad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| House | Easy to hold, quiet street | Small; 2 doors and many windows |
| Farmhouse | Garden and animal space, a well | Far from stores; seen from the road |
| Church | Thick walls, a bell tower lookout | Big open room is hard to hold |
| School gym | Lots of room for settlers | Many doors; easy to find |
| Motel | Many small rooms to fall back through | Faces the highway; raiders know it |
| Gas station | Food and fuel nearby | Glass front; hard to keep secret |

## What you can build

**Builds aren't carried.** You stand where you want something in the hideout and build it there, from parts in your bag or storage. Big finds like a mattress take 2 slots to carry home, or you drag them (slow and loud). Builds stay behind when you move, though some can be stripped for parts.

| Build | Needs | Time | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Board-up | Scrap wood, nails, hammer | 30 min | Blocks a door or window; takes damage |
| Reinforced door | 2 board-ups' worth, a hinge set | 2 hours | Much tougher; can still be opened from inside |
| Can alarm | Tin cans, rope | 15 min | Rattles when something walks into it; wakes you |
| Spike trap | Scrap wood, nails | 1 hour | Hurts the first thing that steps on it |
| Fire trap | Firebomb, rope | 30 min | Burns a doorway when tripped; very loud |
| Lookout | Scrap wood, rope, a roof or tower | 3 hours | See farther; warns of attacks an hour early |
| Rain barrel | A barrel, a tarp | 1 hour | Free water when it rains. Water counts as a little food (there's no thirst meter), cleans wounds and waters the garden |
| Real bed | A mattress, scrap wood | 1 hour | Sleep heals more and lifts SAN a little |
| Stove | Metal scrap, a pipe | 2 hours | Cook indoors; small smoke |
| Radio corner | A radio, batteries | 30 min | Warnings, rumors, the mixtape |
| Storage crate | Scrap wood, nails | 30 min | More storage slots |
| Root cellar | A shovel, scrap wood | 6 hours | Food spoils slower inside |
| Lockbox | Metal scrap, a lock | 1 hour | Raiders can't take what's in it |
| Blackout covers | Rags or blankets, nails | 30 min a window | No light shows at night |
| Workbench | Scrap wood, a vise or tools | 2 hours | Needed for big crafts |
| Garden plot | A shovel, seed packets | 2 hours | Grows food by season |
| Coop or pen | Scrap wood, wire | 3 hours | Keeps hens or a goat |

**Wear depends on the build.** Boards weaken in rain and traps get used up. Beds, stoves, cellars and storage only break in attacks.

## What brings an attack

Each night, the game rolls for an attack. The odds start low and rise with:

- Noise you made near the hideout that day.
- Light at night and cooking smoke by day.
- How many days in a row you've stayed.
- Whether raiders know where you live (the secret hideout rules).
- A horde passing through the section.

Quiet, careful players see an attack about once a week. Loud ones see one every few nights.

## How an attack plays out

**Everyone at the hideout fights.** Your companions take orders as usual. Each settler has a post you set ahead of time (a door, a window, the lookout) and follows the order rules from there. They can be hurt or killed. More people means more hands, but also more mouths and more noise.

1. **Woken:** a can alarm, a companion on watch or a Light Sleeper wakes you. Without any of those, the attackers get a free turn.
2. **Set up:** one free turn to place companions and grab a weapon.
3. **The fight:** turn by turn inside your building. Zombies push at boards; raiders try doors and windows and may shout.
4. **Twists:** one per attack, drawn from the list below. A big attack can rarely get 2.
5. **The end:** when attackers are dead, flee, or you slip out the back.

Attacks last about 3 to 5 minutes of play. A small attack your defenses clearly win is settled in one line ("Two dead pushed at the back door and gave up.").

**Twists (first list):**

- A weak board gives way early.
- A companion panics (their fear is in play).
- Raiders shout a deal through the door: food for leaving you alone.
- The fire trap spreads to a room you need.
- A child is with the raiders.
- A second group arrives halfway through.
- The power flickers on and lights the whole house.
- A zombie was already inside.

**Choices beyond swinging:** fall back a room, light a fire trap, hold a door with your back (costs health), pay the raiders off, slip out the back with what you carry.

## When you're away

If an attack comes while you're out, it's worked out from your defenses and any settlers: boards take damage, traps go off, some stored items may be lost, and a settler may be hurt. You see a short report when you get back.

## Settlers' jobs

Settlers each take one job: guard, garden, cook, craft or scavenge nearby. Each needs a meal a day. Guards make attacks start in your favor; gardeners water crops so they don't fail.
