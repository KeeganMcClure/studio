# Life After weather and events

Part of the Life After bible (`bible.md`, Time and seasons). Set Nov 23 from Keegan's review of the weather and events draft. Weather is grounded: a radio warns a day ahead, and the sky is less sure. Exact numbers are tuned in playtests.

## Weather

Each day has one weather. Most days are clear or cloudy. This table is the base for the starting region (the Midwest farm county). Each new region changes the mix.

| Weather | Seasons | How often | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clear | All | Most days | Nothing special |
| Rain | Spring, summer, fall | 1 day in 5 | Noise rings shrink by 1; rain barrels fill; tracks wash out |
| Thunderstorm | Spring, summer | 1 or 2 a season | Thunder hides a gunshot; lightning can start a fire; travel is slow |
| Fog | Spring, fall | 1 or 2 a season | You and zombies see 3 tiles less; sneaking is easier |
| Heat wave | Summer | 1 a season, 2 to 3 days | Hunger and sleep drop faster; fresh food spoils in a day; zombies more active |
| Cold snap | Fall, winter | 1 or 2 a season | Without heat you lose health each hour outside |
| Blizzard | Winter | 1 a season, 1 to 2 days | Travel between sections is slow and dangerous, but nobody else is out, so you rarely meet raiders or moving zombies; you can barely see; zombies freeze in place |
| Thaw | Late winter, spring | Once | Frozen zombies wake up over 2 days |

## World events

Events come from what you did and where you are, not on a timer. Each has a rough chance per season once its cause is in play. The list is meant to grow with updates and new regions.

| Event | Cause | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horde passing through | Your noise (see `bible.md`, Rumors and late threats) | 30 to 60 zombies cross the section over a day |
| Raider push | The raider grudge or your wealth | A raid on your hideout or a road you use |
| A stranger at the door | Any time you have a known hideout | Someone asks for help: real, or a raider scout |
| A fire in town | Storms, firebombs, raiders | A building burns; its loot is gone; the smoke draws zombies |
| The power flickers on | Rare, once a year at most | A few buildings light up for a night; it draws everyone |
| A plane overhead | Very rare | Hope, a rumor, and a SAN lift; maybe a drop of supplies |
| Sickness at a camp | A camp you know, in winter | They ask for medicine, or turn people away |
| A trader caravan | You trade often | Traders arrive with rare stock for a day |

## Warnings

| Source | How early | How sure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battery radio | A day ahead | Right 9 times in 10 |
| CB radio | Hours ahead | Right about raiders and hordes nearby, but raiders may lie |
| Reading the sky (anyone) | Morning of | Right about 6 times in 10 |
| Read the sky (Outdoors 5) | A day ahead | As sure as the radio |
| Lookout (built) | An hour ahead | Sure, for attacks only |
