| 2026-11-25 | Slice part B, the town (first half) | **Built** (spec 002, the parts with an approved look, Keegan's call Nov 25): the two sections from the seed (`src/town.js`), each 60 × 60 tiles. The houses have four roads in a loose grid with two cross streets, 12 to 15 homes (ranch, two-story, trailer; some with a garage, a driveway car, a locked door), Lincoln Elementary (classrooms, nurse's office, cafeteria, library, the fire axe case), First Grace Church (pews, the locked office, the basement pantry, a graveyard) and the park with its shed. The highway strip has Route 9 through the middle, Star Fuel with its canopy and pumps, Bargain Barn with the locked sporting goods counter, the Starlite's 8 rooms, a pick of the other stores (Crossroads, Big Bun, Reel Time, Lucky's, Gold Mine Pawn with its locked case, PartsPro, Suds City), 3 or 4 roadside homes, dead cars, a big rig, a billboard, the field. Every place is a building you walk into; its inside map has rooms and search spots (fridge, pantry, cabinets, medicine cabinet, closet, nightstand, workbench, shelves, register, cooler and the rest), cars have a trunk and a glovebox, and bodies can be searched. The clock: 1 minute a step (2 in a duffel bag), searching 10, picking 20, forcing 5, kicking 10, the walk between sections 3 hours (1 by bike); Steady Hands and the store clerk halve their times; days roll over and seasons turn every 14 days. Hunger and sleep drop at the preset's rates (Standard about 2 and 3.3 an hour) and do nothing at zero yet. Day and night: dusk by season, dark through `engine/light.js`, your own few tiles (6 with Night Owl, 2 with Nearsighted), the flashlight's 7-tile cone on batteries, neon on Star Fuel, the Starlite and Big Bun; Nearsighted blurs far things by day. Searching shows what you found on the cardboard list: take one, take all, or leave; a searched spot stops glowing and stays as you left it. Locks: a key, the car keys for a trunk, pick (lockpicks or Fixing 3, 40% plus 10% a Fixing level), force with a crowbar, fire axe or hammer (wears it), or kick a door; forcing and kicking break the lock. Loot follows items.md's places, 1 spot in 3 empty, 0 to 3 items, more on Gentle and less on Harsh (ammo 1.5x and half), 25 to 35 meals of food a section on Standard, at most one loose gun a section plus the counter and the pawn case; keys and car keys turn up elsewhere in the section. Bikes in about 1 in 4 garages. The top bar card with the section or place name on its tab. Start → Wait (1 to 10 hours). Notes for waking up, the first lock, the first full bag, the first dusk, the first road trip and a section nearly picked clean, shown for a moment at the bottom. The map remembers what you've seen and marks places looted or promising. Day 1 starts waking in a bedroom in a random home at 7 AM. Saves: version 2 adds the town's state to `la.run`, and a part A save upgrades by building the town and waking as on day 1. Alive: a stray dog, crows that fly off, litter on the wind, the motel sign flickering, smoke from a far fire, a flag and washing in the wind. **Shared:** `engine/light.js` takes flashlight cones (`cones`, `beam`). **Art:** `assets/town-art.js` (ground, buildings, props, inside maps, the dog and crows). **Checks:** the three sizes and 600 presses; same seed same town; 3 seeds with 15 to 20 places a section, all reachable, joined by the road; each action's time and the time cuts; menus stop the clock; key, pick, force and kick; the town comes back after a reload; a week of looting on seed 4242 left all 18 houses places looted by day 5; night dark but readable at all three sizes with the flashlight; both test saves open (new: `test-saves/2026-11-25-v2-town.json`). Maps of both sections are in `dist/town-houses.png` and `dist/town-strip.png`. **Look check:** the street by day and night and the strip's neon were put side by side with mockup 04 at 375 × 667 and 320 × 480; night was darkened and the flashlight beam brightened to match. **Deviations:** buildings open as inside maps, not a roof fade (spec 002, 1 leaves it to the Developer; how it feels at 320 × 480 is a question for Keegan's phone test). The Starlite's 8 rooms each count as a place, so the motel is always on the strip (without it the strip can't reach 15 places). Route 9's east end is jammed with wrecks, since no section lies that way yet. The houses always have four roads; in some worlds one is only Route 9, so 3 or 4 are residential. A bag you find goes on if you aren't wearing one. A bike waits in its garage until you first ride it, then is parked by the road where you get off. Dark comes on over the hour after dusk and lifts over the hour before dawn. The words of the notes are a first pass (the spec gives when, not what). Starting flashlights come with batteries in. Trees turn in fall and are bare in winter; the rest of the season colors wait for the Designer. Stand-ins until the screens below are drawn: L says how full the bag is, R says how many places you've found and looted, the flashlight is turned on and off from the Start menu, and the Start menu has no Notebook row yet. **Left:** the screens marked To draw need approved mockups first: the bag (L) with eating, using, dropping and piles, reading magazines and the field guide, and crafting on the Recipes page; the map screen (R) with this section's marks and the road map; and the notebook (Start → Notebook). Then parts C and D. |
