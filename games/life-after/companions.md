# Life After companions

Part of the Life After bible (`bible.md`, Companions). Set Nov 23 from Keegan's review of the companions draft. Companions use the skills, traits, jobs and looks parts in `survivor.md`. Exact numbers are tuned in playtests.

## Party size

You plus up to 2 companions travel together. Once you have a bigger hideout, a 3rd companion can join the party (Keegan, Nov 23). A bigger party fights better but makes more noise and eats more. What counts as a bigger hideout gets set in the spec that adds companions.

## Who a companion is

- **One main skill at 3**, from the same 8 skills as your survivor, plus 1 or 2 other skills at 1. Some companions are better than others. Stronger survivors (a main skill of 4, or more skills) are rarer and slower to join, so they need more trust first.
- **A balanced set of traits** from the survivor lists (good points never more than bad points).
- **One fear and one want** from the lists below.
- **Looks, age and attraction** from the same parts as your survivor. Some are open to romance with you, some aren't.
- **A past job** from the 12 jobs, which sets their main skill and what they talk about.
- **They level up.** Companions grow skills with use and unlock abilities at levels 3 and 5, the same way your survivor does.

## Fears

Facing their fear costs them SAN, and they may refuse an order that walks into it. Helping them face it over evening talks lifts bond. With enough of that, the fear can go away for good, but it's hard and doesn't always work.

| Fear | When it bites |
| --- | --- |
| The dark | Going out at night, unlit rooms |
| Being left alone | Holding a spot alone, short jobs |
| Fire | Torches, firebombs, burning buildings |
| Crowds of the dead | 4 or more zombies in sight |
| Closed spaces | Basements, crawlspaces, cars |
| Deep water | The lake, crossing rivers, boats later |
| Blood | Treating wounds, gore, butchering |
| Dogs | Strays, a guard dog, Find the dog |
| Losing someone again | When you or another companion is badly hurt |
| Being found | Loud noise, a known hideout, raiders on the radio |

## Wants

A want gives them their own reason to stay. Helping with it lifts bond a lot. Ignoring it for a long time makes them restless.

- Find a missing sister, brother or child (ties to rumors).
- Get back to their hometown. At low bond they may leave to go alone. At Easy or Close bond they ask you to take them, which becomes a road trip. Once there, they come back with you or stay, and their hometown becomes a place to visit and trade.
- Give someone they lost a proper burial.
- Find a working guitar (or another thing they loved before).
- Learn a skill from you (to reach 3 in something).
- Stay put: a safe place they never have to leave.
- Get even with a raider gang.
- Quit drinking or smoking.
- Find out if their old church or camp still stands.
- Keep a kid safe (if they meet or have one).

## Bond with you

Bond is a hidden number from 0 to 100. You never see it; the Party menu shows only a word.

- **Starting bond:** 20 to 40. Higher if you saved them, lower if you traded hard or they saw you kill.
- **Words:** Wary under 30, Easy 30 to 69, Close 70 and up.
- **Leaving:** under 10, they may walk out, steal or side with raiders.

| Raises bond | About | Lowers bond | About |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Share food when food is short | +5 | Eat while they go hungry | -8 |
| Save them in a fight | +10 | Leave them behind | -20 |
| Keep a promise | +8 | Break a promise | -12 |
| Let them rest when hurt | +3 | Send them out hurt | -5 |
| Help with their want | +10 to +20 | Ignore their fear | -5 |
| An evening talk that goes well | +3 to +6 | Kill someone they asked you to spare | -15 |

## Order rules

Without an order, a companion checks these in order every turn and does the first that fits:

1. If badly hurt (under 25 health), back away toward you or cover. Once nothing is within 4 tiles, bandage themselves from their own bag.
2. Never step next to 2 or more zombies.
3. If something is attacking you or them, hit it.
4. While sneaking, never shoot and never break things.
5. Stay inside your noise ring and within 4 tiles of you.
6. Otherwise, follow you one step behind.

Orders from Select: **Follow**, **Hold here**, **Sneak**, **Fight**. Low bond: they may hesitate a turn on Fight or Hold. Close bond: they also guard you while you sleep and team up on attacks.

## Short jobs

Send one companion out alone. Before you send them, you see the job, the time it takes and their chance.

| Job | Time | Base chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scavenge a known place | 3 hours | 70% |
| Hunt or fish | 4 hours | 60% |
| Scout a section | 6 hours | 65% |
| Trade with a group you know | 4 hours | 75% |

- **Chance goes up** with the right skill (+5% a level), daylight and a quiet section. It goes down at night (-20%), when they're hurt (-15%), and in a dangerous section (-10% to -25%).
- **Results:** the chance shown is the chance of full success, with loot and a short story. For a 70% job, the other 30% splits about like this: partial with less loot 15%, back hurt 10%, missing 4% (they may return in 1 to 3 days, or not), dead 1%.
- **Deaths stay under 5%** on ordinary jobs. Only very risky jobs (a raider camp, a horde section) go higher, and they move more of the rest toward hurt and missing too.

## Bonds between people

Everyone at the hideout, companions and settlers, has a hidden bond with each other. Traits push it (two Kind people warm up; a Hothead and a Stubborn clash), and so do events (one saves the other, one eats the other's share). Close pairs fight better side by side and take short jobs together. Clashing pairs argue at evening talks and won't hold a spot together. A clashing pair of settlers may fight, and one may leave.

## Personal arcs

Each companion gets one arc built from parts: a setup, three scenes that open as bond reaches 40, 60 and 80, and a payoff.

**Setup seeds:** a missing sibling; a past as a raider; a debt to a dangerous man; a child they left behind; a crime they hide; a lost faith; a hidden illness; a promise to a dying friend.

**Payoffs:** a big SAN lift for everyone, a found person who may join as a settler, a skill raised to 4, a keepsake, or a hard end (the sister didn't make it) that still closes the arc.

## Evening scenes

Short scenes with 2 or 3 choices. First set of seeds:

- Playing cards for the last chocolate bar.
- Fixing a radio together and catching a voice.
- Teaching them to shoot, or to sew a wound.
- They ask what you did before.
- A nightmare: they wake shaking.
- Splitting a found bottle of whiskey.
- A birthday they almost forgot.
- Arguing about whether to help a stranger.
- Showing them a keepsake.
- Watching the stars from the roof.

## Romance

- Opens only with an adult companion whose attraction fits you, at Easy bond or better.
- A flirt choice at an evening talk: if they're open, bond rises; if not, it drops a little, and for some it drops a lot.
- **Pace** (set Nov 25, 2026: it depends on the person). Each companion open to romance has one, rolled with their other parts and hidden like bond:
  - **Quick:** falls fast. Romance scenes at bond 35, 50 and 65.
  - **Steady:** scenes at bond 50, 70 and 90.
  - **Slow:** takes a long time to trust anyone. Scenes at bond 65, 80 and 95.
  - About 1 in 4 are Quick, 1 in 2 Steady and 1 in 4 Slow. The last scene fades to black whatever the pace.
- A partner lifts SAN at every shared evening, and losing them costs 50 SAN.
